Protection and privacy

  • Filter many known ad, tracker, and malicious domains with DNS rules.
  • Choose protection modes and manage filter lists and custom rules.
  • Browse with the built-in Privacy Browser.
  • Review local DNS activity and protection statistics.
  • Use Smart Boost for on-device, rule-based maintenance suggestions.

DNS filtering has limits and may not block ads served from the same domains as app content.
